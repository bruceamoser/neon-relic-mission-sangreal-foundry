# Investigator photographs — GM handoff

24 optional snapshots: 16 mundane subjects and 8 visible observations. No hidden symbols, text puzzles, background watchers, or extra evidence are intended. Use alongside the existing detailed clue images.

Share a card only when players photograph the subject with appropriate access and matching conditions. The images are illustrative viewpoints, not authoritative architectural plans or proof of capture time. NPC pictures show appearance only. The empty hangar is for after the aircraft departs. GM classifications and inference limits are not player text.

Restart Foundry after deploying the rebuilt module so the new pack is registered, then use the existing Content Installer. Cards appear under Investigator Photos. No world documents have been imported automatically.

## Where the pack is wired in

- **Walkthrough cues.** Each investigation beat carries an *If the investigators take photographs:* paragraph with clickable links to the cards reachable at that point — vault approach and descent (C01, C24), vault interior (C02, C03, C22), Corvi's quarters (C04–C06), Geneva bank (C07–C09), Sonnenberg (C10–C12, C23), Lingotto (C13–C15), San Nazzaro (C16–C18), the airfield after departure (C19, C20), and the council chamber (C21). The cues are optional colour, never a required beat.
- **GM index.** The walkthrough's last page, *Investigator Photos — GM Index*, lists all 24 cards grouped by location and marks each one **mundane** or **observation**. That classification appears there and in the cards' GM notes only — never in player-facing text.
- **Card data.** `foundAtSlugs` links each card to its location document (the field the information web and the card's *Found At* tab read), and the four NPC frames carry `npcSlugs` for the person photographed. C19/C20 have no location link: the airfield is not one of the seven dossier sites, so their condition lives in the GM notes. Player-facing names stay neutral — the four named staff appear as their visible role (clerk, priest, woman in a white coat, escort), and the GM index names them.
- **Naming.** The cards are **C01–C24** ("camera"), a prefix used nowhere else, so a link label like `C04 — Shelves beside the window` can never be confused with the case board's off-camera person tracks (P1–P7) or the F-series photographic evidence.
- **Not clues.** None of the 24 cards is referenced by the information web, and none is a fallback packet. The detailed evidence cards keep their examination requirements (I01, I15, I20, I39, I45 are cross-referenced in the relevant GM notes).

All files are native-size WebP at q85, totaling 3.89 MB. PNG masters and generation prompts remain outside the module build. Built-in ImageGen produced the images. User QA is pending.
